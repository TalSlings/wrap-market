# FAQ content plan

Checklist for the full Hebrew FAQ. Keep these anchors stable because the listing form and listing pages link to them.

- [ ] `#sizes`: complete size table, measurement caveats, ring-sling and scrap terminology.
- [ ] `#sizes`: “How do I know which size fits me?” with common carries and base-size guidance.
- [ ] `#gsm`: what GSM measures, where to find it, and what the ranges mean in practice.
- [ ] `#materials`: natural, artificial, synthetic and other fibers.
- [ ] `#materials`: vegan, natural and easy-care marketplace badge rules.
- [ ] `#pricing`: how to price a second-hand wrap; availability, condition, defects, demand and comparable listings.
- [ ] `#condition`: the complete condition scale and the separate role of defect disclosure.
- [ ] Color uncertainty: words, screens, lighting and photographs can describe the same color differently.
- [ ] Color structures: text and yes/no example images for stripes, ombré, rainbow and positive/negative designs.
- [ ] Beginner babywearing FAQ: what to learn first and where to find meetings, workshops and individual guidance with instructors.
- [ ] Add curated external information sources when they have been reviewed.
